**Edmond**: <message to="cl-ment">
*Abono de família* is Portugal's monthly child allowance, paid by Segurança Social for each dependent child up to age 16 (or 24 if in education).

*How eligibility works*
It's means-tested. The government calculates your household's *rendimento de referência* — essentially your annual income divided by a coefficient based on family composition (each adult counts as 1.0, each child ~0.30). There are 3 income brackets:

```
Bracket 1 (lowest income)  → highest monthly amount
Bracket 2 (middle)         → reduced amount
Bracket 3 (higher income)  → small amount
Above ceiling              → not eligible
```

The exact thresholds are indexed to the IAS (currently ~522€/month). The 2025 ceiling for bracket 3 is roughly *20× IAS annualised* — around *145k€/year* for the household before the per-capita adjustment.

*Your situation*
Given your combined income (Clément ~53k€/year salary + Lola's salary + bonuses), you likely fall into *bracket 3* — eligible but with a small monthly amount per child. You wouldn't be above the ceiling unless total household income is very high.

You need to apply through *segurancasocial.pt* or a Loja do Cidadão. Required documents:
• NIF of both parents and the child
• Proof of residence in Portugal
• Last IRS declaration
