# Granola — Gordon Ramsay (Clément's version)

**Source**: Gordon Ramsay (adapted by Clément)
**Prep time**: 10 min
**Cook time**: 30 min
**Effort**: Low
**Batch-friendly**: Yes — Clément scales to large batches (several weeks supply)
**Age-appropriate**: Adults, older kids (nuts — supervise younger children)

---

## Modifications vs. original (Clément's version)
- Added **olive oil** alongside honey
- Added **cinnamon** (canela moída)
- Added **chia seeds**
- Added **goji berries** (on top of raisins + cranberries, not replacing them)
- Uses **breakfast seed mix** (linseed, sesame, poppy seeds) in addition to individual seeds

## Instructions

1. Melt honey and vanilla together (microwave or stovetop).
2. Mix oats, puffed rice, almonds and seeds together.
3. Pour honey (and olive oil) over dry mix and stir well.
4. Spread onto a shallow baking tray in a thin layer.
5. Bake at 180°C for 25–30 min, stirring occasionally to prevent burning.
6. Remove from oven, stir in dried fruit.
7. Cool completely before storing in a sealed container.
