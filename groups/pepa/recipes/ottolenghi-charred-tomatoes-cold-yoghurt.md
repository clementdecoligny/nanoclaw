# Hot Charred Cherry Tomatoes with Cold Yoghurt

**Source**: Yotam Ottolenghi — *Simple*
**Prep time**: 5 min
**Cook time**: 15–20 min
**Effort**: Very low
**Batch-friendly**: No — must be served immediately (hot/cold contrast is the point)
**Age-appropriate**: Adults, older kids (spice can be reduced/omitted for kids)
**Family verdict**: ❤️❤️❤️ — family favourite

---

## Why this works for this family
- Uses Greek yoghurt (always in stock)
- Tomatoes + olive oil + garlic — pantry staples
- Ready in 20 min, zero skill required
- Spectacular contrast: scorching hot tomatoes on fridge-cold yoghurt
- Works as starter, light dinner with bread, or side

---

## Ingredients (serves 2–4 as starter / light meal)

| Ingredient | Amount | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Cherry tomatoes | 350 g | |
| Olive oil | 3 tbsp | Add at end of cooking where possible (per nutrition plan) |
| Cumin seeds | ¾ tsp | |
| Sugar | ½ tsp | |
| Garlic cloves | 3 | Whole, unpeeled |
| Fresh thyme | 3 sprigs | |
| Fresh oregano | 3 sprigs + extra leaves for serving | |
| Lemon | 1 | 3 strips of peel + 1 tsp zest |
| Flaked salt | ½ tsp | |
| Black pepper | to taste | |
| Greek-style yoghurt (extra thick) | 350 g | Must be straight from the fridge — cold is essential |
| Urfa chilli flakes | 1 tsp | Or ½ tsp regular chilli flakes |
| Bread | to serve | |

---

## Method

1. Preheat oven to **220°C fan** (very hot — you want char).
2. Toss tomatoes with olive oil, cumin seeds, sugar, garlic, thyme, oregano sprigs, lemon peel strips, salt and pepper in a mixing bowl.
3. Spread on a baking tray. Roast **15–20 min** until tomatoes are charred and bursting.
4. While tomatoes roast: spread cold yoghurt on a wide platter (with a lip), making a dip in the centre with the back of a spoon.
5. When tomatoes come out of the oven: immediately spoon them over the yoghurt with all their juices, garlic, herbs and lemon skin.
6. Finish with picked oregano leaves and Urfa chilli flakes.
7. Serve **at once** with bread.

---

## Key rule
The contrast between scorching hot tomatoes and fridge-cold yoghurt is everything. Do not let either warm up. Tomatoes straight from oven → platter → table.

---

## Notes
- Urfa chilli flakes are mild and smoky — worth having in stock. Substitute: regular chilli at half dose.
- Can prep the tomato mix (step 2) ahead and refrigerate — roast to order.
- Keeps badly — do not attempt to store leftovers. Make exactly what you'll eat.
